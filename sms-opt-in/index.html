FLOSYS SMS Opt-In

End users opt in to FLOSYS SMS Notifications on this page (or an equivalent intake/booking form that shows the same language and fields). This page is the public consent experience for carrier review.

The checkbox is unchecked by default. The phone number field is required to complete opt-in. Consent is voluntary and is not required to purchase FLOSYS services or complete a job booking.

Other opt-in methods

Text keyword: Users may also opt in by texting START, YES, or SUBSCRIBE to our Twilio messaging number. They receive a confirmation reply with FLOSYS brand name, purpose, frequency, message-and-data-rates disclosure, STOP/HELP, and Privacy/Terms links before enrollment is active.

Verbal: During booking or onboarding, a FLOSYS representative or AI receptionist reads the same disclosures. The end user must give an affirmative verbal yes. The SMS destination is the number they provide or confirm on the call; consent is recorded in account notes.
