FLOSYS SMS Opt-In

End users opt in to FLOSYS SMS Notifications on this page (or an equivalent intake/booking form that shows the same language). This page is the public consent experience for carrier review.

The checkbox is unchecked by default. Consent is voluntary and is not required to purchase FLOSYS services or complete a job booking.

Users may also opt in by texting START, YES, or SUBSCRIBE to our Twilio messaging number, or by giving verbal yes during booking after hearing the same disclosures.
