Opt in to FLOSYS SMS

End users opt in to FLOSYS SMS Notifications on this page, or on an equivalent intake or booking form that shows the same phone field, unchecked checkbox, and disclosure language. Messages are recurring texts about missed-call follow-up, appointment reminders, booking confirmations, and customer-care updates.

The checkbox is unchecked by default. Check it only if you want these texts. The phone number field is required to complete opt-in. Consent is voluntary and is not required to purchase FLOSYS services or complete a booking.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Other opt-in methods

Text keyword

Users may text START to (346) 296-3380. YES and SUBSCRIBE sent to that same number are also opt-in keywords. The confirmation reply is:

FLOSYS: You are opted in. Msg&data rates may apply. Msg frequency varies. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.

Verbal

During booking or onboarding, a FLOSYS representative or AI receptionist reads the script below in full. The end user must give an affirmative verbal yes. Silence or “no” is not consent. The SMS destination is the number they provide or confirm on the call. Consent is recorded in account notes.

Verbal script (read in full)

You are speaking with FLOSYS. Our business address is 28718 Hidden Cove Drive, Magnolia, Texas 77354. You can reach us at 346-590-6353 or kirby@flosys.org. Our website is https://flosys.org/.

We would like your permission to send recurring SMS text messages from FLOSYS about missed-call follow-up, appointment reminders, booking confirmations, and customer-care updates. Message frequency varies, up to several messages per week. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to unsubscribe, or reply HELP for help. Consent is voluntary and is not required to purchase FLOSYS services or complete a booking. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Our SMS Privacy Policy is at https://flosys-org.github.io/a2p-legal/sms-privacy/ and our SMS Terms are at https://flosys-org.github.io/a2p-legal/sms-terms/.

Do you agree to receive these text messages from FLOSYS at the mobile number you are providing on this call? Please say yes to opt in, or no if you do not want texts.
