Program description

By opting in to FLOSYS SMS Notifications, you agree to receive recurring text messages from FLOSYS related to our AI receptionist and customer-communication systems for construction and trades businesses. Messages may include missed-call follow-up, appointment reminders and confirmations, booking confirmations, booking updates, customer-care replies, and related service notifications.

Consent

You can opt in by:

  • Checking an SMS opt-in box on a FLOSYS or customer website form (not pre-checked), or
  • Providing verbal consent by phone during booking / intake, or
  • Texting a keyword such as START, YES, or SUBSCRIBE to (346) 296-3380

Users who text START to (346) 296-3380 receive this confirmation: “FLOSYS: You are opted in. Msg&data rates may apply. Msg frequency varies. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.”

Consent is not a condition of purchasing FLOSYS services or completing a job booking. Consent is voluntary.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. You may receive up to several messages per week depending on your activity (for example, after a missed call or around an appointment). Conversational replies may result in additional messages.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Check your mobile plan for details. FLOSYS does not charge a separate fee for standard SMS participation, but your carrier may.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP to cancel. After you send STOP, you will receive a confirmation and no further messages from this program unless you opt in again.

Reply HELP for help. You can also call 713-398-0947 or email kirby@flosys.org.
